# THE PILL — Session Prep

In-app / onboarding text. Adapted from the original binaural tips file.  
No drug language. No “hallucination.” No medical claims.

---

## Before you press play

Headphones on. This is stereo / immersive audio — one ear alone won’t carry it.

1. Clear the small stuff first: bathroom, messages, hunger. Your body will interrupt you if you don’t.
2. Get comfortable. Soft surface. Nothing tight or itchy.
3. Don’t rehearse the outcome. You’re starting a sound session, not waiting for a miracle.
4. Slow your breathing until you feel quieter.
5. Optional warm-up: **Start Again** or **Ease Off**. Let leftover thoughts run out while you check volume and cable.
6. Silence the phone. Dim the lights. Block stray light if you want it deeper.
7. Keep wires off your skin so a tickle doesn’t fake intensity.

If you’re new, start **LIGHT** or **REGULAR**. Save the densest capsules for later.

---

## Entering

8. Press play while still. Let the sound land before you move.
9. Lie back if you want. Soft cover over the eyes — leave room to blink. Darkness helps; staring at nothing with effort does not.
10. Uncross your legs. Soft arms. Hands resting, not clenched.
11. Optional: count backward from a random mid number (like 847). Something for attention to hold — not a race.
12. If counting loops or slips, let it. Start another number if you hit zero and still feel outside.
13. Picture each number as you count. Soft focus is enough.
14. Notice pulse in the chest or belly. Soften into it. Don’t force a feeling.
15. Pulses may get clearer, slower, or farther. Stay with the session.

---

## Deep state

16. When attention softens, stop managing. Let the session run.
17. Don’t chase results mid-session. Judge it after.
18. Twitches and small shifts are fine. Correcting them wakes the mind.
19. If the sound gets quiet or distant, stay. You’re still in it.
20. Light intention is okay — *floating*, *rocking* — as imagination, not a promise.
21. Eyes closed: dots, color, dark field. Small shifts are normal. Don’t demand a movie.
22. When the track ends, lie still a few minutes. The after-quiet is part of it. Replay only if you want — the second pass is often easier.

---

## Safety

- Don’t drive or do anything unsafe during or right after a deep session.
- Not medicine. Not a substitute for care.
- If it feels wrong, stop. Switch to **CALM**, **GROUND**, or **Start Again** · LIGHT.
