Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-03-29 THE PILL (“we”, “the app”) stores your profile and listening data on your device using local storage. We do not operate a backend that receives your name, favorites, history or ratings. Data stored on device may include: display name, mood answers, listening journal, ratings, downloaded library, membership flag and language preference. We do not collect an email address. You can delete this data anytime with Delete account in Profile. Signing out clears the active profile on this device. Subscriptions (THE PILL+) are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Purchase history and entitlement status are managed by RevenueCat on your device. RevenueCat privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy Audio experiences are not medical treatment. Contact: support@thepill.app
