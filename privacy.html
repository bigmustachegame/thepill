Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-03-29 THE PILL (“we”, “the app”) stores your profile and listening data on your device using local storage. We do not operate a backend that receives your name, email, favorites, history or ratings in this build. Data stored on device may include: display name, email address, mood answers, listening journal, ratings, favorites, membership flag and language preference. You can delete this data anytime with Delete account in Profile. Signing out clears the active profile on this device. Audio experiences are not medical treatment. If you connect future cloud features (streaming, accounts, payments), this policy will be updated and a public HTTPS copy will be published for App Store / Play Console. Contact: support@thepill.app
